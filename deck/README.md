# Deck: the Assignment 4 talk

One HTML file, the same player as our Assignment 3 talk: slides drawn on a canvas, step by step, with
speaker notes that give a time to finish each slide by.

    node deck/tools/data.js      # src/data.js from results/ (and deck/facts.json)
    python3 deck/build.py        # dist/turkish-draughts-a4.html, fonts and data inlined; opens from disk

Keys: → or Space next step, ← back, N speaker notes (with a clock), P print the speaker script, F full screen,
R replay the step, 1–9 jump to a slide, T restart the clock.

| path | what |
|---|---|
| `src/scenes.js` | the slides; every number comes from `src/data.js` or a file named in the slide's footer |
| `src/data.js` | generated by `tools/data.js`: the ladder, the ablation, Monte Carlo, the draw rules in the search (results/fifty, V11), quiescence with the A3 weights and the SPRTs of PVS and the extension (results/arena.csv, their intervals from docs/decisions.md), endgame table sizes and the engine's size, the endgames of one minute (results/minute), `facts.json` |
| `facts.json` | moves and compulsory captures per position, counted over replayed ablation games (`tools/data.js LOGDIR`, see its header) |
| `src/core.js`, `src/player.js`, `src/style.css` | drawing helpers and the player, from the Assignment 3 talk |
| `fonts/`, `img/` | the fonts (latin and latin-ext subsets) and the team logo, inlined by `build.py` |

`dist/` is not committed: it is built from the files here, so it cannot fall behind them.

## Checks behind slide 7 (what we left out of our C++ agent)

- Threads in the browser: a page posts a shared `WebAssembly.Memory` to a worker, in Chromium 141 (Playwright).
  Opened from disk and served over plain HTTP, `crossOriginIsolated` is false, `SharedArrayBuffer` is undefined and
  `postMessage` throws `DataCloneError: ... SharedArrayBuffer transfer requires self.crossOriginIsolated`. Served
  with `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin` and `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp`, the worker gets
  the memory. Our site is one file opened from disk, so it cannot be isolated.
- Rust: threads in WebAssembly for browsers need a nightly compiler, a rebuilt standard library (`build-std`),
  `+atomics,+bulk-memory` and cross-origin isolation (wasm-bindgen-rayon, docs.rs).
- Lazy SMP: Chess Programming Wiki, Shared Hash Table: searches gain from probing entries other searches wrote;
  "the gains come from the effect of nondeterminism", and entries are not written atomically.
- Endgame tables: `tools/data.js` counts every position with up to n pieces as docs/roadmap.md does (men off their
  first and last rank, kings anywhere, both sides with a piece, either side to move, mirror and colour symmetry,
  2 bits a position): up to 4 pieces 42 M positions (10 MB), 5 pieces 1.9 G (450 MB), 6 pieces 68 G (16 GB),
  7 pieces 2.0 T (460 GB), in MB and GB of 2^20 and 2^30 bytes. The engine (`site/pkg/dama_web_bg.wasm`) is 0.25 MB.
